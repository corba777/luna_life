The checks that now run on every experiment came from these faults: the raw bytes of the request actually sent are compared with the reviewed template; a masked second-round request is checked before any third-round request goes out; each round counts the cells that called the model.

Predictions that failed

  • One agent with nine examples would reach 0.80 (0.56).
  • The channel would lift reasoning populations by at least 0.30 in four of five worlds (three).
  • A nine-example agent with ten rounds would end at least 0.10 below the population (it ended 0.28 above).
  • After the fault was found: the four publishing cells had made no difference (the difference is 3.7 standard errors).
  • gpt-oss-120b, held out: the predicted levels for rounds 10–20 missed (2 of 8 runs inside their bands; the predicted ranking held by sign, p = 0.46 and 0.20 to round 40, not significant); all three locked predictions for the continuation to round 80 failed.
  • gpt-oss-20b: the topology ranking held in worlds 1 and 3 and failed in world 5.
  • Claude Haiku 5.5: predicted class stubborn, measured graded; no population run inside its predicted level band (0 of 8).
  • Claude Haiku 5.5 without the channel: the share on the true rule would lie in 0.159–0.209, derived from the lattice runs' witness transition rates (0.127; witnesses left the true rule 2.4 times as often as on the lattice). Locked with it and held: every run without the channel below every complete-graph run with it (p = 1/70).
